The Subjunctive: Doubt & Impersonal

Grammar

The subjunctive of doubt

The indicative states what we treat as real; the subjunctive marks what is doubted, denied or merely possible. After a main clause expressing uncertainty, the verb in the que-clause shifts to the subjunctive.

Dudo que venga hoy. — I doubt he'll come today.
Es posible que llueva mañana. — It may rain tomorrow.

Certainty vs. doubt — the contrast

Indicative (certainty)Subjunctive (doubt)
Creo que tiene razón.No creo que tenga razón.
Es verdad que está aquí.No es verdad que esté aquí.
Estoy seguro de que viene.No estoy seguro de que venga.

Notice how negation flips it: a positive verb of opinion (creo que, pienso que, es evidente que) takes the indicative, but its negative form expresses doubt and takes the subjunctive.

Impersonal expressions

Impersonal phrases built on es + adjective + que normally require the subjunctive when they convey possibility, necessity or value judgement:

  • Es posible que lo sepan. — It's possible they know it.
  • Es necesario que firmes hoy. — It's necessary that you sign today.
  • Es imprescindible que llegues a tiempo. — It's essential that you arrive on time.
  • Conviene que esperemos. — It's advisable that we wait.

Exception: impersonals of certainty stay in the indicative — es evidente que, es cierto que, está claro que: Es cierto que funciona.

Quizá, tal vez, acaso

These adverbs of doubt may take either mood. The subjunctive stresses greater uncertainty: Quizá venga (less sure) vs. Quizá viene (more sure). Placed after the verb, the indicative is normal: Viene, quizá.

Pronunciation tip

Linking across words (sinalefa): in fast speech a final vowel fuses with the next initial vowel. Es posible que sounds like es-po-si-ble-que with que blending into the following word: que esté → ques-té.

Subjunctive endings carry meaning: the difference between habla and hable, viene and venga is a single vowel — pronounce that final -e or -a cleanly, since it is the only signal that you mean the subjunctive.

Reading: La decisión incierta

No es seguro que el proyecto salga adelante este año. Los inversores dudan que el mercado se recupere a corto plazo, y por eso piden más garantías. Es posible que la empresa tenga que reducir el riesgo antes de firmar nada. El director niega que haya problemas graves, pero pocos se fían de sus palabras. Quizá convenga esperar unos meses, aunque tal vez perdamos la oportunidad. Es imprescindible que el equipo presente una hipótesis clara sobre los costos. No creo que los números mientan, pero parece mentira que nadie los haya revisado con cuidado. Lo único cierto es que, sin certeza, cada decisión supone un salto al vacío.

Translation

It is not certain that the project will move forward this year. Investors doubt that the market will recover in the short term, and so they ask for more guarantees. It is possible that the company will have to reduce the risk before signing anything. The director denies that there are serious problems, but few trust his words. Perhaps it is advisable to wait a few months, although maybe we will lose the opportunity. It is essential that the team present a clear hypothesis about the costs. I don't think the numbers lie, but it seems unbelievable that no one has reviewed them carefully. The only certain thing is that, without certainty, every decision is a leap into the void.

Culture note

The subjunctive is one of the features that most clearly separates a fluent speaker from an advanced learner. In everyday Latin-American speech it is alive and constant: a waiter says ¿Quiere que le traiga la cuenta?, a friend warns No creo que valga la pena, a news anchor reports Es probable que el gobierno anuncie medidas. Far from being a textbook relic, it is the natural grammar of doubt, hope and politeness. Mastering it lets you sound measured and diplomatic — Spanish speakers often soften assertions with quizá or tal vez plus the subjunctive rather than stating things bluntly.

Talk about it

  • ¿Crees que sea sensato esperar antes de invertir, o piensas que conviene actuar ya? — Do you think it's sensible to wait before investing, or do you think it's advisable to act now?
  • ¿Por qué dudan los inversores que el mercado se recupere pronto? — Why do the investors doubt that the market will recover soon?
  • ¿Es posible que una empresa elimine todo el riesgo, o siempre queda algo de incertidumbre? — Is it possible for a company to eliminate all risk, or does some uncertainty always remain?
  • ¿En qué situaciones es imprescindible que alguien asuma la responsabilidad de una decisión? — In what situations is it essential that someone take responsibility for a decision?
  • ¿Te parece mentira que tantas decisiones se tomen sin datos claros? ¿Por qué ocurre? — Does it seem unbelievable to you that so many decisions are made without clear data? Why does it happen?
