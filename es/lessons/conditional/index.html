The Conditional & Polite Requests

What you'll learn

  • Form the conditional of regular and key irregular verbs.
  • Soften requests, advice and wishes with the conditional.
  • Express hypothetical situations: what you would do.
  • Distinguish the conditional from the future and the past.
  • Use polite formulas like ¿podría…?, me gustaría and querría.

Dialogue

Cliente

Buenas tardes. ¿Podría ayudarme con una reserva?

Good afternoon. Could you help me with a reservation?

Recepcionista

Claro que sí. ¿Para cuándo la querría?

Of course. For when would you want it?

Cliente

Me gustaría una habitación para el viernes, de ser posible.

I'd like a room for Friday, if possible.

Recepcionista

Tendríamos una con vista al mar. ¿Le interesaría?

We would have one with a sea view. Would it interest you?

Cliente

Sería perfecta. ¿Sabría decirme el precio?

It would be perfect. Would you be able to tell me the price?

Recepcionista

Costaría noventa dólares la noche, sin el desayuno.

It would cost ninety dollars a night, without breakfast.

Cliente

¿Podría incluir el desayuno? No me gustaría salir a buscarlo.

Could you include breakfast? I wouldn't want to go out to find it.

Recepcionista

Lo haría con gusto. ¿Le importaría dejar una tarjeta como garantía?

I'd do it gladly. Would you mind leaving a card as a guarantee?

Cliente

Por supuesto. Yo en su lugar haría lo mismo.

Of course. In your place I'd do the same.

Recepcionista

Se lo agradezco. Quedaría todo confirmado, entonces.

I appreciate it. Everything would be confirmed, then.

Grammar

What the conditional is for

The conditional (el condicional) is the would tense. Use it to be polite, to give advice, to talk about hypothetical situations, and to express wishes. In English it is usually would + verb: I would travel, it would cost.

Formation — one ending set for all verbs

Like the future, the conditional is built on the whole infinitive. You just add the endings below — and they are the same for -ar, -er and -ir verbs.

Polite requests

  • ¿Podría…? = Could you…? — softer than ¿Puede…?
  • Me gustaría… = I would like… — softer than Quiero…
  • ¿Le importaría / Le molestaría…? = Would you mind…?
  • Deberías… = You should… — gentle advice.

Hypotheticals

Use the conditional to imagine: Yo en tu lugar estudiaría más (In your place I would study more). Con más tiempo, viajaríamos a Perú (With more time, we would travel to Peru).

Pronunciation tip

That accented í carries the stress. In every conditional form the voice lands on the í: ha-bla-RÍ-a, co-me-RÍ-as, ten-DRÍ-a-mos. Keep the vowels around it clean and short so the stressed í stands out.

Don't blur -ía into one syllable. -ía is two beats: EE-ah. haría = ah-REE-ah, not a-RYA. The written accent splits the vowels apart.

Reading: Un viaje que me encantaría

Si tuviera más dinero, viajaría por toda Sudamérica. Empezaría en Colombia y bajaría poco a poco hasta Argentina. Me gustaría quedarme en hostales pequeños para conocer a gente local. Comería en mercados y aprendería a cocinar platos nuevos. Mis amigos dirían que es una locura, pero yo lo haría sin dudar. Algún día, con un buen presupuesto, ese sueño sería realidad.

Translation

If I had more money, I would travel all over South America. I would start in Colombia and slowly make my way down to Argentina. I would like to stay in small hostels to meet local people. I would eat in markets and learn to cook new dishes. My friends would say it's crazy, but I would do it without hesitating. Someday, with a good budget, that dream would be a reality.

Culture note

In Latin America, politeness leans heavily on the conditional and on the formal usted. Walking into a shop, a bank or a hotel, you'll rarely hear a bare command; instead you get ¿Me podría…? or ¿Sería tan amable de…? Softening a request this way signals respect, and answering in kind marks you as courteous rather than abrupt.

Phrases like Me gustaría and ¿Le molestaría? also smooth over money matters — asking about a presupuesto or leaving a propina. When in doubt, reach for the conditional: it almost always sounds more amable.

Talk about it

  • Si pudieras viajar a cualquier país, ¿adónde irías y qué harías allí? — If you could travel to any country, where would you go and what would you do there?
  • ¿Qué consejo le darías a alguien que empieza a aprender español? — What advice would you give to someone starting to learn Spanish?
  • ¿Cómo pedirías un favor importante a tu jefe de manera amable? — How would you politely ask your boss for an important favor?
  • Con un presupuesto ilimitado, ¿qué cambiarías de tu vida diaria? — With an unlimited budget, what would you change about your daily life?
