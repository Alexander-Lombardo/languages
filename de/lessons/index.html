How this course works

  • Every lesson explains the grammar in plain English first, then gives you vocabulary with pronunciation, a dialogue, a reading and a culture note.
  • Exercises are interactive — fill-in, multiple choice, translation, conjugation, sentence building, matching and listening — with instant feedback.
  • Every word and dialogue line has native-quality audio; finished lessons feed a spaced-repetition flashcard deck.
  • Nothing to install, works offline once loaded, progress saved in your browser. Goes all the way to C2 — not just the basics.

The six CEFR levels

A1 · Beginner

Survival basics: the sounds of German, greetings, numbers, family, ordering food, the accusative case and modal verbs.

13 lessons

A2 · Elementary

Describe people, talk about the past (Perfekt & Präteritum), the dative case, routines, separable verbs, work and health.

12 lessons

B1 · Intermediate

Handle two-way prepositions, subordinate clauses, adjective endings, the future, genitive, relative clauses and polite Konjunktiv II.

10 lessons

B2 · Upper-Intermediate

Use hypotheticals, the passive, reported speech (Konjunktiv I), report and debate, switch register, discuss society in detail.

10 lessons

C1 · Advanced

Argue with nuance, handle extended attributes and modal particles, read media and literature, choose words with precision.

10 lessons

C2 · Mastery

Command register, style, the German-speaking varieties, rhetoric, irony and near-native subtlety.

10 lessons
