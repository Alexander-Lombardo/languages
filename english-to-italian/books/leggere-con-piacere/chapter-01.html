Personal pronouns and essere

Use the present tense of essere to say who someone is. Put non before the verb to make it negative: io non sono (I am not), tu non sei (you are not).

SingularPlural
io sono — I amnoi siamo — we are
tu sei — you are (familiar)voi siete — you all are
Lei è — you are (formal)Loro sono — you all are (formal, rare)
lui è / lei è — he is / she isloro sono — they are

In the reading you may also see egli and ella for “he” and “she,” or essi and esse for “they.” Esso and essa can refer to an animal or thing according to its grammatical gender. For impersonal “it is,” use è alone.
