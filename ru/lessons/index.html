How this course works

  • Every lesson explains the grammar in plain English first, then gives you vocabulary with pronunciation, a dialogue, a reading and a culture note.
  • Exercises are interactive — fill-in, multiple choice, translation, conjugation, sentence building, matching and listening — with instant feedback.
  • Every word and dialogue line has native-quality audio; finished lessons feed a spaced-repetition flashcard deck.
  • Nothing to install, works offline once loaded, progress saved in your browser. Goes all the way to C2 — not just the basics.

The six CEFR levels

A1 · Beginner

Read Cyrillic, greet people, introduce yourself, handle numbers, and use your first cases and verbs.

14 lessons

A2 · Elementary

Use all six cases, talk about the past and future, master verb aspect, and describe daily life.

10 lessons

B1 · Intermediate

Handle verbs of motion, give commands, use the conditional, narrate, and manage travel and work.

10 lessons

B2 · Upper-Intermediate

Use participles and gerunds, decline numerals, debate, report speech, and discuss society.

10 lessons

C1 · Advanced

Argue with nuance, use discourse markers and word-formation, read media and literature.

10 lessons

C2 · Mastery
