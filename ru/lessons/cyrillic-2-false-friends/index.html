Grammar

Seven dangerous letters

These seven letters are false friends: each one is the spitting image of a Latin letter, but the sound is somewhere else entirely. Learn them on purpose now, slowly, so your eye doesn't keep tripping later.

Why your eye lies to you

Read С-О-К and an English brain wants to say «cock» or «sock». It is neither — it is sok (juice), because С is [s] and О is [o]. Likewise Р-О-Т is not «rot» the English word but rot with a rolled R, meaning «mouth», and Н-О-С is nos (nose), never «hoc».

The fix is mechanical: cover the word, name each Cyrillic letter by its sound, then blend. Trust the sound, distrust the shape.

Your alphabet so far

With Lesson 00 (А, К, М, О, Т) plus today's seven, you can now read twelve letters: А В Е К М Н О Р С Т У Х. That is enough for dozens of real words.

Pronunciation tip

Roll the Р: Russian Р is a trilled / tapped r, like Spanish perro or Italian Roma. The tip of the tongue taps the ridge behind your upper teeth. Practise рот, рука, метро.

Х is guttural: it is not an English «h» and not «ks». Make friction at the back of the mouth, as in German Bach or Scottish loch. Try хорошо.

The five eye-traps: say them out loud as a drill — В=[v], not b · Н=[n], not h · Р=[r], not p · С=[s], not k · У=[oo], not y.

Е already hides a [y]: at the start of a word or syllable it is [ye] (as in yes). You'll meet more of these «iotated» vowels next lesson.

Culture note

Why do these letters look Latin yet sound different? Both alphabets descend from Greek. Cyrillic borrowed Greek letter shapes directly — so Greek rho (Ρ) became Russian Р [r], and Greek nu (Ν) is the ancestor of Н [n]. English took the same shapes through Latin, but often pinned different sounds to them. The shapes match by accident of history; the sounds never had to.

For English readers this is the single biggest hurdle in week one. Learners who power through real words — сок, рот, метро — instead of just letter charts overwrite the «English reflex» fastest. Say each word out loud; muscle memory beats willpower.
