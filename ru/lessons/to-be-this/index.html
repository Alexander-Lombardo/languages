“To Be”, Это & Simple Sentences

Open the interactive lesson — free

What you'll learn

  • Build present-tense sentences without any word for «am/is/are».
  • Use это to mean «this is / that is» when pointing something out.
  • Make any sentence negative by placing не before the word.
  • Recognise the past forms был/была/было/были and a light future с буду.
  • Use есть and нет to say something exists or does not exist.

Dialogue

Мария

Привет! Кто это?

Hi! Who is this?

Сергей

Это Иван. Он студент.

This is Ivan. He is a student.

Мария

А ты? Ты тоже студент?

And you? Are you also a student?

Сергей

Нет, я не студент. Я врач.

No, I'm not a student. I'm a doctor.

Мария

Правда? А это твой дом?

Really? And is this your house?

Сергей

Да, это мой дом. Он новый.

Yes, this is my house. It is new.

Мария

А это машина? Это твоя машина?

And is this a car? Is it your car?

Сергей

Нет, это не моя машина.

No, this is not my car.

Мария

Здесь есть кафе?

Is there a café here?

Сергей

Да, кафе есть. Оно там.

Yes, there is a café. It's over there.

Мария

А магазин? Здесь есть магазин?

And a shop? Is there a shop here?

Сергей

Нет, магазина здесь нет.

No, there is no shop here.

Мария

Иван русский?

Is Ivan Russian?

Сергей

Да, он русский. Он был в Москве.

Yes, he is Russian. He was in Moscow.

Мария

А ты? Ты был в Москве?

And you? Were you in Moscow?

Сергей

Нет, я не был. Но я буду там завтра.

No, I wasn't. But I will be there tomorrow.

Мария

Здорово! Это интересно.

Great! That's interesting.

Сергей

Да! Завтра будет хороший день.

Yes! Tomorrow will be a good day.

Grammar

Russian has no «am / is / are» in the present

The verb «to be» simply disappears in the present tense. You put two things side by side and the «is» is understood. Often a dash stands in for it in writing.

RussianLiterallyMeaning
Я студент.I studentI am a student.
Он врач.he doctorHe is a doctor.
Москва — город.Moscow — cityMoscow is a city.

Это = «this is / that is»

Это introduces or points at something. It does not change for gender — one word covers «this is», «that is», «these are».

RussianMeaning
Это книга.This is a book.
Это мой дом.This is my house.
Кто это?Who is this?

Don't confuse it with the pronoun он/она/оно: Это книга. Она новая. — «This is a book. It is new.»

Saying «not»: just add не

Put не directly in front of the word you want to deny. Я не студент. = «I am not a student.» Это не моя машина. = «This is not my car.»

есть and нет — there is / there isn't

есть means «there is / there are». Its opposite, нет, means «there is no» (and also just «no»).

RussianMeaning
Здесь есть кафе.There is a café here.
Здесь нет кафе.There is no café here.

A peek at past and future

«To be» does appear in the past and future. In the past it agrees with gender: был (masc.), была (fem.), было (neut.), были (plural). In the future you'll meet буду / будешь / будет. We'll drill these fully later — for now just recognise them.

Pronunciation tip

это is EH-ta: the stressed first э is a clear open «eh», and the final unstressed о softens to [a].

не is a quick, soft nye — the н is palatalised, almost «ny». Keep it light and unstressed; it leans onto the next word.

была stresses the end (bee-LA), while был is one short syllable. Hear how the stress shifts between the genders.

Culture note

The missing «to be» trips up every beginner, but it's a gift: Я студент, Он врач, Москва — город — just name the two things and you have a sentence. In careful writing a dash often marks the spot where «is» would go, especially between two nouns.

When you answer a yes/no question, Russians frequently echo the key word: «Ты студент?» — «Да, студент» or «Нет, не студент». A bare да or нет works too, but echoing sounds warm and natural.
