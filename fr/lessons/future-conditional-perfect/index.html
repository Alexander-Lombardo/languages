futur antérieur & conditionnel passé

Open the interactive lesson — free

What you'll learn

  • Form the futur antérieur (future of avoir/être + past participle) to say an action WILL HAVE been completed before another future moment.
  • Use the futur antérieur after quand, dès que, lorsque, une fois que to mark which future action finishes first.
  • Form the conditionnel passé (conditional of avoir/être + past participle) to say what WOULD HAVE happened.
  • Express regret and reproach with the conditionnel passé (j'aurais aimé..., tu aurais dû...).
  • Build the third conditional: si + plus-que-parfait → conditionnel passé, and recognise the journalistic conditional for unconfirmed news.

Dialogue

Nadia

Tu auras fini le rapport avant la réunion de demain ?

Will you have finished the report before tomorrow's meeting?

Karim

Oui, je l'aurai terminé ce soir. Dès que j'aurai relu la conclusion, je te l'enverrai.

Yes, I'll have finished it tonight. As soon as I've reread the conclusion, I'll send it to you.

Nadia

Parfait. Honnêtement, tu aurais dû me prévenir que le délai était si court.

Perfect. Honestly, you should have warned me that the deadline was so tight.

Karim

Tu as raison, j'aurais dû t'en parler plus tôt. Je suis désolé.

You're right, I should have spoken to you about it sooner. I'm sorry.

Nadia

À ta place, j'aurais demandé un report. On aurait eu une semaine de plus.

If I were you, I'd have asked for an extension. We'd have had an extra week.

Karim

Si j'avais su que c'était possible, je l'aurais fait tout de suite.

If I'd known it was possible, I'd have done it right away.

Nadia

Bon. Une fois que tu auras envoyé le fichier, on le relira ensemble.

Okay. Once you've sent the file, we'll review it together.

Karim

D'accord. Au fait, tu as vu les infos ? Un incendie aurait détruit l'entrepôt voisin.

Alright. By the way, did you see the news? A fire reportedly destroyed the warehouse next door.

Nadia

Quoi ? Selon les premières sources, il n'y aurait pas eu de blessés, j'espère.

What? According to early sources, there were apparently no injuries, I hope.

Grammar

Two compound tenses, one formula

Both new tenses follow the same recipe as the passé composé: auxiliary (avoir or être) + past participle. The only thing that changes is the tense of the auxiliary. Put the auxiliary in the future and you get the futur antérieur; put it in the conditional and you get the conditionnel passé. Agreement rules (être verbs, preceding direct object with avoir) work exactly as in the passé composé.

1. Le futur antérieur — "will have done"

This tense describes a future action seen as already completed by a later point in the future. J'aurai fini avant midi. (I will have finished before noon.)

Pronounfinir (avoir)partir (être)
j' / jeaurai finiserai parti(e)
tuauras finiseras parti(e)
il / elleaura finisera parti(e)
nousaurons finiserons parti(e)s
vousaurez finiserez parti(e)(s)
ils / ellesauront finiseront parti(e)s

Its signature use is after quand, dès que, lorsque, une fois que to show which of two future actions happens first. The completed action takes the futur antérieur; the later one takes the simple future.

  • Quand tu auras terminé, appelle-moi. (When you have finished, call me.)
  • Dès que j'aurai relu la conclusion, je te l'enverrai.
  • Une fois qu'elle aura récupéré, elle s'organisera.

Note: where English uses the present after "when" ("when you have finished"), French requires a future-zone tense — here the futur antérieur.

2. Le conditionnel passé — "would have done"

Same recipe, but the auxiliary is in the conditional. J'aurais aimé venir. (I would have liked to come.)

Pronounaimer (avoir)venir (être)
j' / jeaurais aiméserais venu(e)
tuaurais aiméserais venu(e)
il / elleaurait aiméserait venu(e)
nousaurions aiméserions venu(e)s
vousauriez aiméseriez venu(e)(s)
ils / ellesauraient aiméseraient venu(e)s

Three core uses:

  • Regret: J'aurais aimé être là. (I would have liked to be there.)
  • Reproach: Tu aurais dû me prévenir. (You should have warned me.) — devoir in the conditionnel passé = "should have"; pouvoir = "could have" (tu aurais pu m'appeler).
  • Unconfirmed news (le conditionnel journalistique): L'accident aurait fait trois blessés. (The accident reportedly caused three injuries.)

3. The third conditional: si + plus-que-parfait → conditionnel passé

To talk about an unreal past — what would have happened if the past had been different — pair the two new structures with the plus-que-parfait in the si-clause:

Si-clause (the condition)Main clause (the result)
plus-que-parfait (avais/étais + p.p.)conditionnel passé (aurais/serais + p.p.)
Si j'avais su,je serais venu.
Si tu étais parti plus tôt,tu n'aurais pas raté le train.

Never use a conditional form directly after si: write Si j'avais su, never Si j'aurais su. The condition is always in the plus-que-parfait; only the result carries the conditionnel passé.

Pronunciation tip

The auxiliaries are where futur antérieur and conditionnel passé sound apart. aurai (oh-RAY, future) ends in a closed "ay", while aurais / aurait / auraient (oh-REH, conditional) all share an open "eh" — and the final -s and -ent are silent, so j'aurais, il aurait and ils auraient sound identical. Context tells you the difference.

Make the liaison in j'aurai_eu and link nous_aurons (noo-zoh-ROHN). Keep dû as a tight rounded "dew", distinct from du — same lips, just longer and stressed in tu aurais dû.

Reading: Le projet qu'on aurait pu sauver

Lundi, Élodie s'est dit : « Quand j'aurai terminé ce dossier, je pourrai enfin partir en vacances. » Elle était sûre qu'elle aurait fini avant le délai. Mais une fois qu'elle aura compris l'ampleur du travail, il sera déjà trop tard. En effet, elle avait sous-estimé l'échéance. Si elle avait commencé une semaine plus tôt, elle aurait rendu le projet à temps et elle n'aurait pas passé la nuit au bureau. Son collègue, lui, le lui avait dit : « Tu aurais dû me prévenir, je t'aurais aidée. » Élodie le sait maintenant : à sa place, elle aussi aurait demandé du renfort. Le lendemain, la presse locale parlait d'une autre entreprise : selon les sources, un retard de livraison aurait coûté un contrat important à un concurrent. Élodie a souri. Une fois qu'elle aura récupéré, elle se promet une chose : la prochaine fois, dès qu'un délai sera fixé, elle s'organisera tout de suite.

Translation

On Monday, Élodie told herself: "When I've finished this file, I'll finally be able to go on holiday." She was sure she would have finished before the deadline. But once she realises the scale of the work, it will already be too late. Indeed, she had underestimated the due date. If she had started a week earlier, she would have handed in the project on time and she wouldn't have spent the night at the office. Her colleague had told her so: "You should have warned me, I would have helped you." Élodie now knows it: in his place, she too would have asked for backup. The next day, the local press was talking about another company: according to sources, a delivery delay reportedly cost a competitor an important contract. Élodie smiled. Once she has recovered, she promises herself one thing: next time, as soon as a deadline is set, she'll get organised right away.

Culture note

The conditionnel journalistique is a daily feature of French news. When facts aren't yet confirmed, reporters shift the verb into the conditional to flag distance: L'incendie aurait fait plusieurs victimes, le suspect aurait pris la fuite. There's no neat one-word English equivalent — translators reach for "reportedly", "allegedly" or "is said to have". Spotting this conditional tells you a French source is hedging rather than asserting.

The reproachful tu aurais dû ("you should have") is also a small cultural marker. Used bluntly it can sting, so French speakers often soften it with à ta place ("if I were you") or a conditional of politeness: tu aurais peut-être pu... Mastering these forms lets you give feedback and express regret with the nuance a B2 speaker is expected to command.

Talk about it

  • Qu'est-ce que tu auras accompli avant la fin de l'année ? — What will you have accomplished before the end of the year?
  • Raconte une décision que tu aurais prise différemment. — Tell me about a decision you would have made differently.
  • Si tu avais su parler français plus tôt, qu'est-ce que tu aurais fait ? — If you had known how to speak French earlier, what would you have done?
  • Qu'est-ce que tu feras une fois que tu auras terminé ce cours ? — What will you do once you have finished this course?
  • Y a-t-il quelque chose que tu aurais dû dire à quelqu'un, mais que tu n'as pas dit ? — Is there something you should have said to someone but didn't?
