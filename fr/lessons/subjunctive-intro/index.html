The Subjunctive: Wishes & Emotions

What you'll learn

  • Form the present subjunctive of regular verbs from the ils/elles stem.
  • Use the key irregular subjunctives (être, avoir, aller, faire, savoir, pouvoir, vouloir).
  • Express wishes and will with vouloir que, souhaiter que, il faut que.
  • Express emotions with être content/triste/heureux que and avoir peur que.
  • Choose between the subjunctive and the infinitive depending on the subject.

Dialogue

Camille

Alors, tu as enfin décidé ? Je veux que tu me dises tout !

So, have you finally decided? I want you to tell me everything!

Yanis

Oui. Il faut que je parte à Lyon le mois prochain pour le nouveau poste.

Yes. I have to leave for Lyon next month for the new job.

Camille

C'est génial ! Je suis tellement contente que tu aies cette chance.

That's wonderful! I'm so glad you have this opportunity.

Yanis

Merci. J'ai un peu peur que ce soit difficile au début, loin de tout le monde.

Thanks. I'm a little afraid it will be hard at first, far from everyone.

Camille

C'est normal. Mais il vaut mieux que tu essaies plutôt que de regretter.

That's normal. But it's better that you try rather than regret it.

Yanis

Tu as raison. Je souhaite vraiment que ça marche.

You're right. I really hope it works out.

Camille

Je veux que tu réussisses, et je suis sûre que tu vas y arriver.

I want you to succeed, and I'm sure you'll manage it.

Yanis

Ça me touche. J'aimerais que tu viennes me voir bientôt.

That touches me. I'd like you to come see me soon.

Camille

Bien sûr ! J'ai hâte que tu sois installé pour visiter la ville.

Of course! I can't wait for you to be settled so I can visit the city.

Yanis

Parfait. Je suis heureux que tu me soutiennes autant.

Perfect. I'm happy that you support me so much.

Grammar

What is the subjunctive?

The subjunctive (le subjonctif) is a verb mood used to express things that are not stated as plain facts: wishes, wills, emotions, doubts, and necessity. It almost always appears after que when there are two different subjects.

1. Forming the regular subjunctive

Take the ils/elles form of the present indicative, drop the -ent to get the stem, then add the endings -e, -es, -e, -ions, -iez, -ent.

Example with parler: ils parlent → stem parl- → que je parle, que nous parlions.

3. Triggers: wish, will, emotion

  • Wish / will: vouloir que, souhaiter que, aimer que, il faut que, il vaut mieux que.
  • Emotion: être content/heureux/triste que, avoir peur que.

Example: Je veux que tu partes. (I want you to leave.) — Il faut qu'elle fasse ses devoirs. (She has to do her homework.)

4. Same subject? Use the infinitive

If the subject does not change, drop que and use the infinitive instead:

  • Two subjects → subjunctive: Je veux que tu partes.
  • One subject → infinitive: Je veux partir.
  • One subject → infinitive: Je suis content de partir. (with emotion + de)

Pronunciation tip

Many subjunctive forms sound identical to the indicative for the singular and the third-person plural (que je parle sounds like je parle). The clear giveaways are the nous/vous forms (parlions, parliez) and the irregulars like soit, aille, fasse, puisse.

Listen for the soft glide in soit ("swah") and aille ("eye"), and the doubled ss sound in fasse ("fass") and puisse ("pweess"). These short, distinctive words are your strongest clue that the subjunctive is in play.

Reading: Le grand projet de Sofia

Sofia rêve depuis longtemps d'ouvrir sa propre boulangerie. Ses parents veulent qu'elle finisse d'abord ses études, mais elle a peur que le temps passe trop vite. "Il faut que je commence maintenant", dit-elle souvent à ses amis. Sa meilleure amie, Léa, l'encourage : "Je suis contente que tu aies un vrai projet. Il vaut mieux que tu essaies que de rester avec des regrets." Sofia est un peu déçue que la banque hésite à lui prêter de l'argent, mais elle ne perd pas espoir. Elle souhaite que ses gâteaux rendent les gens heureux. Le soir, elle prépare son plan et imagine son avenir. Elle veut réussir, non pas pour devenir riche, mais pour le bonheur de créer quelque chose. Ses amis ont hâte que la boulangerie ouvre enfin ses portes.

Translation

Sofia has dreamed for a long time of opening her own bakery. Her parents want her to finish her studies first, but she's afraid time is passing too fast. "I have to start now," she often says to her friends. Her best friend, Léa, encourages her: "I'm glad you have a real plan. It's better that you try than stay with regrets." Sofia is a little disappointed that the bank is hesitating to lend her money, but she doesn't lose hope. She wishes for her cakes to make people happy. In the evening, she prepares her plan and imagines her future. She wants to succeed, not to become rich, but for the happiness of creating something. Her friends can't wait for the bakery to finally open its doors.

Culture note

The subjunctive sometimes feels intimidating to learners, but the French use it constantly in everyday speech without thinking about it. Expressions like il faut que ("I/you have to") are among the most common ways to talk about obligations, far more frequent than the formal devoir in casual conversation.

While careful written French maintains the subjunctive after many triggers, spoken French is gradually simplifying it: some speakers replace it with the indicative after verbs of opinion in the negative, or avoid it entirely by rephrasing with an infinitive. Still, mastering the core forms (soit, ait, fasse, puisse, aille) is essential, since these are heard everywhere from the news to the dinner table.

Talk about it

  • Qu'est-ce que vous voulez que vos amis fassent plus souvent ? — What do you want your friends to do more often?
  • Êtes-vous content(e) que le week-end arrive ? Pourquoi ? — Are you glad the weekend is coming? Why?
  • Quel est un projet que vous souhaitez réaliser cette année ? — What is a plan you wish to carry out this year?
  • De quoi avez-vous peur qu'il arrive dans le futur ? — What are you afraid might happen in the future?
  • Que faut-il que vous fassiez avant la fin du mois ? — What must you do before the end of the month?
