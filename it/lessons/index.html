How this course works

  • Every lesson explains the grammar in plain English first, then gives you vocabulary with pronunciation, a dialogue, a reading and a culture note.
  • Exercises are interactive — fill-in, multiple choice, translation, conjugation, sentence building, matching and listening — with instant feedback.
  • Every word and dialogue line has native-quality audio; finished lessons feed a spaced-repetition flashcard deck.
  • Nothing to install, works offline once loaded, progress saved in your browser. Goes all the way to C2 — not just the basics.

The six CEFR levels

A1 · Beginner

Survival basics: greet people, introduce yourself, handle numbers, food and simple questions.

10 lessons

A2 · Elementary

Talk about the past (passato prossimo & imperfetto) and routines, shop, describe weather and health, express likes.

10 lessons

B1 · Intermediate

Use the future and conditional, the particles ne/ci and object pronouns, narrate, give opinions, handle travel and work.

10 lessons

B2 · Upper-Intermediate

Debate, report speech, master the congiuntivo, the passive and si-constructions, discuss society in detail.

10 lessons

C1 · Advanced

Argue with nuance, use the periodo ipotetico, discourse markers and idioms, read media and literature.

10 lessons
